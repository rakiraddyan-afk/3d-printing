What we do

From broken original to printed replacement

Every part is rebuilt around your original, not a generic mold.

3D Scanning

We digitize your broken or worn part with precision structured-light scanning, capturing every curve and mounting point.

CAD Reconstruction

Broken pieces are digitally repaired and modeled to the part's original geometry and tolerances before printing.

Precision Printing

Printed layer by layer in UV-stable, impact-resistant materials matched to the part's original job under the hood or dash.

Fit-Check & Finish

Each part is sanded, dyed to match, and dry-fit tested against reference drawings before it ships to you.

The process

Four steps from cracked to complete

01

Send us the part

Mail your original (even in pieces) or send clear photos with measurements and your car's make, model, and year.

02

We model it

Our team scans and reconstructs the part in CAD, restoring worn or missing geometry to spec.

03

We print & finish

Printed in a durable material suited to the part, then cleaned, finished, and quality-checked by hand.

04

Delivered to your door

Your new part ships worldwide, ready to install, with the digital file kept on file for reorders.

Materials

Matched to the job, not one-size-fits-all

ASA / ABS

UV & heat resistant

Our default for exterior and dashboard parts, holding color and shape under sun and engine-bay heat.

Reinforced Nylon

High impact strength

Used for brackets and clips that take repeated stress or vibration, tougher than the factory original.

Resin (SLA)

Fine detail finish

For badges, switch surrounds, and trim where sharp edges and a smooth, paint-ready surface matter most.

Why 2LIFE

Keeping old European iron on the road

Parts bins for cars built before the 2000s are drying up. Dealers stop stocking, suppliers stop tooling, and the plastic that's left turns brittle with age. We built 2LIFE to solve exactly that problem: a small-batch, print-on-demand parts shop for the pieces nobody else will make anymore.

Every job starts with your part, not a catalog, so what you get back fits the way the original did, panel gaps and all.

  • No minimum order. We print a single clip as readily as a full trim set.
  • Files kept on record. Reorder a part in seconds if it breaks again.
  • Worldwide shipping. Small, light parts ship affordably almost anywhere.
  • Owner & enthusiast run. We restore our own classics too.
